Why Flat Feet Need Special Shoes

Runners with flat feet experience excessive pronation (inward foot rolling), which can lead to knee pain, shin splints, and plantar fasciitis. According to podiatrist Dr. Michael Chen at the American College of Sports Medicine, approximately 20-30% of the population has flat feet, requiring specialized footwear.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need custom orthotics?

Not necessarily. Research from the American Podiatric Medical Association shows that 65% of flat-footed runners find sufficient support from stability shoes alone. Custom orthotics are recommended only for severe cases or chronic pain.

How often should I replace my shoes?

Replace running shoes every 400-500 miles or every 6 months, whichever comes first. The midsole foam loses 40% of its cushioning properties after 400 miles according to a 2023 study.

Can flat feet be corrected?

While adult flat feet cannot be fully "corrected," strengthening exercises can improve arch function by 15-20% over 3 months, based on physical therapy research.
